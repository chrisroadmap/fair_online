Underlying data

Climate simulations use FaIR v2.2, calibrated and constrained against observations and CMIP6 with the fair-calibrate v1.6.0 ensemble (FASTMIP Phase 1; CMIP7 historical forcing 1750–2023; IGCC 2024 temperature constraints). Emissions pathways are the ScenarioMIP-CMIP7 scenario categories used in that calibration. Observed temperature and greenhouse gas concentration overlays are from Indicators of Global Climate Change 2025 (Climate Indicator Project). This tool is for exploration and teaching — not a substitute for the full assessed literature (IPCC AR6/AR7) when making decisions.
